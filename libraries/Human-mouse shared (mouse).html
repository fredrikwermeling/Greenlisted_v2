Human–mouse shared is not a published library. It collects the sgRNAs whose 20-nt (or 19-nt) spacer is identical in at least one human and at least one mouse library in the Validate sgRNA index, about 13,000 guides, searched here by mouse gene symbol. Most target the same gene in both species, in sequence the two genomes share, so one guide can be used in human and mouse cells. Columns give the libraries in each species, the human gene the guide is annotated to, and whether it is the same gene. Guides are ordered by how many libraries pick them. Many genes have no shared guide, and those that do often have only one or two.


Cite the libraries the guides you use come from (listed per guide). Each library checked its guide against its own genome, so the PAM is there in both, but the guide may cut a different exon or transcript in the other species. Open Context in each species before relying on it there.
